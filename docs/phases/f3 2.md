# F3 — AI employee builder

Branch `phase/f3-builder`.

## What was built

- **Employees** (`/employees`): cards with initials, role, channel, languages, status (live pulses) and an "Unpublished changes" tag. With nobody hired yet, the one showpiece of the screen: a large invitation to hire.
- **Hire** (`/employees/new`), four steps:
  1. Channel (Instant, Bulk, Inbound) and languages: an opening language, English always on, and one more if wanted.
  2. The brief, typed or spoken. Speaking records 16 kHz mono from the microphone with a live waveform; every 25 s (and on stop) a WAV chunk goes to `/ai/transcribe-chunk` and its text is appended in order.
  3. The AI's follow-up questions; answer any, skip the rest.
  4. The draft materialises card by card (numbered 01–0n, 700 ms reveal, 90 ms stagger), then "Hire" creates the employee and fills its script and captured fields.
  "Write it myself" (or the fallback when the AI has no key, shown as the "Connect" state) creates an empty employee. A failed fill after creating never hires a second employee on retry.
- **Employee detail** (`/employees/:id/...`), tabs as routes:
  - **Script**: welcome line, call flow steps (add, edit, move up/down, delete), when to end, about the business, the permanent FAQ, strictness 1–5, details sent in (pre-call variables) and details to capture (applied at once), and "Change it with AI" whose proposal lands in the editor. Edits autosave as a draft 700 ms after typing stops; actions and the result webhook are never sent, so this editor cannot wipe them.
  - **Voice**: voices that speak all the employee's languages, filtered by tier, with a sample played from the vendor; speed and background sound saved when the slider is released.
  - **Behaviour**: name and role, three style switches (optimistic, rolled back on refusal), go live / pause with the API's reason in place, duplicate, move to another channel, start over and delete, each destructive one behind a dialog that says what it costs.
  - **Versions**: the last three published scripts as a perspective deck that fans out on hover or focus (always fanned under reduced motion); restoring one confirms that it goes live on the next call.
  - **Publish bar**: whenever the draft differs from what is live, "Apply to live calls" or "Discard changes".
- **Shared pieces**: `useEmployeeWrite` (one mutation for every write that returns the employee, with optional optimistic preview and rollback), `useAction` (busy and error for one-off actions), `Confirm`, `Select`, button styles, `lib/list.ts` (move, replace, remove, identifier), `lib/recorder.ts` (mic capture and WAV encoding).

## Fixes to earlier work

- `unwrap` inferred its result type from the variable it was assigned to, so assigning into a `T | null` made the result nullable. Both branches of its return type are now `NoInfer`.
- The publish bar sat inside `<main>`, whose enter animation made it the bar's containing block; it is portalled to `<body>`.
