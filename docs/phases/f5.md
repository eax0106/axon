# F5 — Credits, billing, numbers, KYC, go live

Branch `phase/f5-billing`.

## What was built

- **Billing** (`/billing`, also from the credit chip):
  - Balance in large type, a runway bar (days the balance lasts at the last 30 days' average daily spend, warn colour under a week; it moves only when the numbers change) and a 30-day daily-spend sparkline, both computed from the real ledger (Indian calendar days; refunds and money coming in are not spend).
  - **Add credits**: presets and custom amount from `/billing/rates`; `/wallet/topup/order`, then Razorpay's own checkout (card or UPI), loaded only when paying; the signed result goes to `/wallet/topup/verify`. "Pending" is said plainly when Razorpay has not confirmed capture yet.
  - **Limits and invoice details**: hourly spend cap (empty clears it) and GST invoice details; the API's GSTIN checks show in place.
  - **Statement** this or last month (opening, each kind with its count, closing), **usage per employee**, **transactions** newest first with running balance and "Show older" paging, and **invoices** with the GST split.
- **Numbers** (`/numbers`): your numbers (type, the employee using it, renewal or grace date, monthly rent, calls at once with a confirm that says what an extra channel costs, release with a confirm that says what is lost); the store (search by digits, quote with first charge and balance, buy). The first purchase walks through holder **KYC**: PAN, then Aadhaar on DigiLocker in a new tab, then "I've finished"; a verified holder is reused. A purchase the carrier did not confirm in time is shown as being checked, as the API reports it.
- **Employee number**: a picker on the Behaviour tab attaches any free number or detaches it; "Get a number" when none is free.
- **First run** on Overview: hire → apply a script → verify your phone → get a number → attach and go live, each step read from the account's real state with a progress bar, the next step highlighted; it disappears when all five are done.

## Notes

- DigiLocker's return address must be https (the API's rule); on a local http stack the step answers with that refusal.
- The Razorpay checkout script is the one third-party script in the app, loaded from Razorpay only when the owner pays.
- The spend sparkline is flat on a new account: nothing has been spent. No sample data is drawn.
